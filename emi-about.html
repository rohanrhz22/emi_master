System Architecture

How EMI Master is structured under the hood

Presentation Layer

Responsive HTML/CSS with dark theme, glassmorphism effects, tab-based navigation, and smooth animations. Works on all screen sizes.

Business Logic Layer

Pure JavaScript engine handling EMI calculations, amortization schedules, timeline projections, and strategy recommendations.

Data Layer

LocalStorage-based persistence with JSON serialization. Planned upgrade to Firebase/IndexedDB for cloud sync and offline support.
